Arvados is a modern open source platform for managing and processing large biomedical data.

## Project Highlights

- Collaborated on UI & UX with design staff
- Focus on ARIA roles for accessibility in sections with tabbed navigation sections
- Developed in [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/)
- Use of CI/CD Pipeline during development
